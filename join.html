The student's research will focus on how hydrologic variability, climate change and human activities shape river and stream ecosystems. Fieldwork will likely center on rivers of the Texas Hill Country.

The core of the project will be quantifying and modelling how streamflow regimes are changing and what those changes mean for river ecosystems, combining long-term gauge and climate records, watershed models and field data. Possible directions include:

  • Watershed hydrologic modelling with SWAT+ or similar models
  • Characterizing flow regimes, environmental flows and streamflow alteration
  • Climate and land-use change impacts on river systems
  • Intermittent streams, drying–rewetting dynamics and hydrologic connectivity
  • Data-driven and statistical modelling of ecohydrological processes

There are opportunities to link hydrology with freshwater biodiversity and ecosystem function, including eDNA-based biodiversity work in the lab. The student will also lead one chapter that uses large-scale existing data or synthesizes existing research.

Who we are looking for

A background in hydrology, water resources engineering, environmental science, earth sciences, ecology or a related field. Experience with hydrologic modelling, GIS, R, Python or statistics is desirable. Strong quantitative skills and an interest in interdisciplinary research are especially welcome. Applicants from all backgrounds are encouraged to apply, and international applicants are welcome.

What the position involves

Mentoring undergraduate researchers, being a collaborative member of the group and taking part in Biology Department activities. The student will teach as a teaching assistant for 4 semesters. Funds are available to attend one national and one regional conference each year.

How to apply

  1. Email schattop@txstate.edu with the subject line "PhD Inquiry – Fall 2027".
  2. Attach your CV and a one-page statement of research interests.
  3. Include contact information for two references.

Review of applications begins December 15, 2026, and continues until the position is filled.
